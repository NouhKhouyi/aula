Understand it until it feels obvious.

aula is a teacher that starts by finding out what you already know, plans the shortest route to what you want to understand, and checks each step lands before building on it.

It starts with what you already know.

An explanation written for everyone fits no one. aula works out where your understanding ends, agrees a route with you, and never builds on a step that hasn't landed.

  1. Probe

    Questions that escalate until something breaks, to find the edge of what you know. Missing is the point, and “I don't know” is always a good answer.

  2. Plan

    The shortest route from what you hold to your goal, drawn as a dependency map. You check the foundations and approve it, or send it back.

  3. Teach

    One reasoning step at a time: predict, explain, check. A step that didn't land is repaired before anything is built on it.

  4. Practise

    Problems that never name the idea they need, so it has to come to mind on its own. Hints respond to your reasoning.

Think out loud.

Every question has a confidence slider and room for your reasoning. The teacher sees why you answered, not only whether you were right — and a confident wrong answer is treated as a misconception to clear up, not a gap to fill.

Not quite

A test is 99% accurate. The disease affects 1 in 10,000 people. You test positive. Roughly how likely is it that you have it?

  1. 1About 1%
  2. 2About 99%
  3. 3About 50%

You were 70% sure.
I keep wanting to say 99% and I know that's the trap, but I can't say why.

Out of 10,000 people, about one is ill and tests positive, while about 100 healthy people test positive anyway. 99% is the chance of a positive test if you are ill — the other way round.

Knowledge that carries forward.

Each session adds what it established to one knowledge graph: the ideas, the reasoning that connects them, and where each came up. The next lesson reads it, so it can probe precisely instead of starting from scratch.

Yours, on your machine.

No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

  • Your model, your key

    Claude, with facts checked on the web; any OpenAI-compatible service; or a model running locally. You pay your provider directly.

  • Plain files

    Sessions, the graph and settings are JSON on your disk, to read, back up or delete.

  • Lessons in your vault

    Save any lesson to Obsidian with typeset math and drawn diagrams, and attach your course notes to set the scope.

  • A method you can read

    How it teaches is written in plain prose in the repository. Change the prose, change the teacher.

Get started

You need Node.js 20.9 or newer. Add an API key in Settings, or choose demo mode to try a lesson without one. 

git clone https://github.com/NouhKhouyi/aula
cd aula
npm install
npm run build && npm start
# then open http://localhost:3000
