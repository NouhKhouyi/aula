Understand it until it feels obvious.

aula is an agent harness for learning. It finds where your understanding ends, plans the shortest route to what you want to understand, and teaches one checked reasoning step at a time. Local-first, open source, with the model of your choice.

Get started View on GitHub

Not an explanation. A route.

Asking a chatbot gets you an explanation pitched at nobody in particular. aula works out where you are, agrees a route with you, and doesn't build on anything that hasn't landed.

  1. Probe

    Questions that escalate until something breaks, to find the edge of what you know — bounded on both sides. Missing is the point; "I don't know" is always a good answer.

  2. Plan

    The minimal route from what you hold to your goal, as a dependency map. You read the foundations and approve it, or send it back.

  3. Teach

    One reasoning step at a time: predict, explain, check. A step that didn't land is repaired before anything is built on top of it.

  4. Practise

    Problems that never name the idea they need, so it has to come to mind on its own. Hints answer your actual reasoning.

Think out loud.

Every question has a confidence slider and a field for your reasoning. The teacher sees why you answered, not just whether you were right — and a confident wrong answer is a misconception to evict, not a gap to top up.

You · 14:31 I keep wanting to say 99% and I know that's the trap, but I can't say why.

Knowledge that carries forward.

Each session adds the ideas it established, and the reasoning steps between them, to one knowledge graph — linked back to where they came up. The next lesson reads it, so it can probe precisely instead of starting from scratch.

Yours, on your machine.

No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

  • Your keys, your provider Claude with web verification, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local model. You pay your provider directly.
  • Plain files Sessions, the graph and settings are JSON on disk that you can read, back up and diff.
  • Lessons in your vault Save any lesson to Obsidian with real typeset math and drawn diagrams. Attach your course notes to set the scope.
  • Readable method The teacher's whole method is a prompt in the repository. Change how it teaches by editing prose.

Get started

You need Node.js 20.9 or newer. Choose demo mode in Settings to try it without a key.

git clone https://github.com/NouhKhouyi/aula
cd aula
npm install
npm run build && npm start
# open http://localhost:3000
