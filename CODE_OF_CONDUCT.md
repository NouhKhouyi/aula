# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a project about learning, and people ask questions here that
expose what they don't know. Make that safe.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a documentation failure, not a
  competence failure.
- Be specific in criticism and generous in interpretation.
- Remember that "I don't understand this" is the most useful thing anyone can
  say here — and the hardest. Answer it well.
- Respect that people learn differently. The pedagogy in this repo is one
  approach, not a verdict on anyone's intelligence.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone's level of
  knowledge.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to
anyone representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Responses, proportionate to severity: a private word, a public correction, a
temporary block, or a permanent ban.
